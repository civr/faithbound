# Realistic look: credits
All photo textures, the tree models and the sky in this folder come from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) and are CC0 (public domain).
They were resized, colour-graded and recompressed for Faithbound.

## Walls, roofs and streets
- r_plaster: plastered_wall_04
- r_wood (oak beams): medieval_wood
- r_planks, r_planks_n: brown_planks_05
- r_stone, r_stone_n: medieval_blocks_02
- r_brick, r_brick_n: castle_brick_07
- r_tiles, r_tiles_n: clay_roof_tiles_02
- r_thatch, r_thatch_n: thatch_roof_angled
- r_cobble: cobblestone_floor_08
- r_dirt: rocky_trail_02
- r_asphalt: asphalt_02
- r_conc (office walls): brushed_concrete
- r_conc2: concrete_layers_02

## Trees
- imp_broadleaf: island_tree_01 (3D model, rendered from sixteen angles)
- imp_fir: fir_sapling (3D model, rendered from sixteen angles)

## Sky
- r_sky: a Poly Haven HDRI sky, tone-mapped to a small picture for the light from above

The window frames, glass, shutters, timber framing and window lights on the house fronts are drawn by the game on top of these photos.
