# Models

All models are by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).
Converted from FBX to glTF binary with FBX2glTF; static vehicles had their animations removed.

- Farm Animals Animated (Jun 2018): cow, horse, sheep, pig
- Animated Men Characters / Animated Women Characters (Feb 2019): male_casual, male_shirt, female_casual, female_dress
- Knight Character Animated (Jul 2018): knightcharacter
- Ship pack (Feb 2018): viking_boat, sail_ship, boatwsail, cruiseship
- Airplane pack (Jan 2017): commercial_airplane, military_airplane, smallplane
- Tank Pack (Jun 2019): tank, tank2, tank3
