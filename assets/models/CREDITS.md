# Models

All models are by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).
Converted from FBX to glTF binary with FBX2glTF; static vehicles had their animations removed.

- Farm Animals Animated (Jun 2018): cow, horse, sheep, pig
- Animated Men Characters / Animated Women Characters (Feb 2019): male_casual, male_shirt, female_casual, female_dress
- Knight Character Animated (Jul 2018): knightcharacter
- Ship pack (Feb 2018): viking_boat, sail_ship, boatwsail, cruiseship
- Airplane pack (Jan 2017): commercial_airplane, military_airplane, smallplane
- Tank Pack (Jun 2019): tank, tank2, tank3
- Nature Pack Essentials vol.3 (Dec 2016): tree_a-d, bush_a-c, rock_a-c
- Civilization Buildings (May 2018): egypt_temple, japan_temple, maya_temple, pantheon, med_house
- Farm Buildings (Sept 2018): farm_* (barns, silos, windmills, water tower, well, chicken coop)
- Buildings Pack (Jan 2019): town2s, town3b, town3s, town4, cottage1
- Buildings pack (Aug 2017): sub_house1-5, flat1, flat2, shop, hospital, bank (palette textures baked into vertex colours)
- Fish Pack Animated (Apr 2018): dolphin, shark, whale, manta_ray, fish1-3
- Farm Animals Animated (Jun 2018): llama, zebra; Animal Pack Vol.2 (Jul 2017): wolf
- Realistic Car Pack (Nov 2018): car1, car2, suv, sports1, sports2, taxi, cop
- Public Transport Pack (Feb 2017): bus, schoolbus
- Train Pack (Apr 2019): loco_front, loco_tender, loco_wagon, hs_front, hs_wagon, cargo_front, cargo_box, cargo_coal

Models that came without colours had them restored from their material names; all were simplified with meshoptimizer for the game.
